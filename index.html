hello

I am Duong, an AI researcher.

I am a thesis-based MSc student in Computing Science at the University of Alberta, advised by Dr. Xi Ye and Dr. Jocelyn Chen.

My goal is to build AI agents that support and align with humans. I study language-model reasoning and develop reliable agents that learn from evolving contexts and interact with people.

Previously, I was an AI Resident at Qualcomm AI Research and VinAI Research.
