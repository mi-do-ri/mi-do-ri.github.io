LongHarness Bench: Stress-Testing Language Model Harnesses for Long-Context Reasoning

Quang Hieu Pham*, Thuy Duong Nguyen*, Jocelyn Qiaochu Chen, Xi Ye

A four-task benchmark that measures the accuracy and cost of long-context agent harnesses. Across tested systems, no model-harness pair led on every task.

200 problems 25 model-harness pairs 12.5× cost gap

ClozeMath: Improving Mathematical Reasoning in Language Models by Learning to Fill Equations

Quang Hieu Pham*, Thuy Duong Nguyen*, Tung Pham, Anh Tuan Luu, Dat Quoc Nguyen

A text-infilling training approach that links natural-language plans to mathematical equations. The method improves accuracy, sample efficiency, robustness, and test-time scaling.
