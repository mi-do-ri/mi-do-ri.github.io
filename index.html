Hi there, I am Duong!

I am a thesis-based MSc student in Computing Science at the University of Alberta, advised by Dr. Xi Ye and Dr. Jocelyn Chen.

My goal is to build AI agents that support and align with humans. I study language-model reasoning and develop reliable agents that learn from evolving contexts and interact with people.

Previously, I was an AI Resident at Qualcomm AI Research and VinAI Research.

Selected Projects

LongHarness Bench

The benchmark tests retrieval and context-management strategies for long-context agents. Similar systems can differ by up to 12.5× in cost.

E-commerce Agent

I built data-curation and evaluation tools for a personal shopping agent. I also developed an FAQ module that reached 95.5% accuracy on a synthetic test set.

Agent-UniRAG

I designed an agent framework for question answering and distilled its knowledge into smaller open-source language models.
